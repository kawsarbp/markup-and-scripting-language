How It Works

Getting started with DIU Bus Tracker is easy. Just follow these four simple steps.

Step 1

Create an Account

Sign up as a student or driver with your DIU information in under a minute.

Step 2

Open the Live Map

Log in and see every active bus and route on an interactive map.

Step 3

Track Your Bus

Watch the bus move in real-time and check its estimated arrival time.

Step 4

Arrive on Time

Plan your departure around the live location and never wait blindly again.

About DIU Bus Tracker

A Simple, Fast and Reliable Commute Helper

DIU Bus Tracker is a lightweight website built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It loads instantly on any device, so both students and drivers can check bus information quickly and easily.

  • Simple, clean interface that works on mobile and desktop
  • Fast-loading static pages with no extra setup required
  • Easy to maintain and run anywhere with just a web server
